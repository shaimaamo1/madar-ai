# MADAR AI commercial pack

## Product presentation

MADAR AI gives real estate teams one view of their customers, property inventory, conversations, and next actions. The value proposition is practical: fewer missed inquiries, more consistent qualification, faster inventory lookup, coordinated viewings, and management reports based on recorded work. It is designed for agencies and brokers in Egypt and the Gulf, with English/Arabic navigation and bilingual inquiry handling.

Suggested presentation sequence: fragmented inbound inquiries → unified customer context → verified property matching → self-service viewing → follow-up accountability → transparent business metrics → configurable company workspace. Show the working website journey before discussing external channels. Describe WhatsApp, Telegram, and n8n as prepared integrations pending live authorization and verification, not connected channels.

## Five-minute sales demonstration

1. Open the interactive demo. Point out the fictional-data banner.
2. Open Integrations → Website chat and submit “I want an apartment in New Cairo, budget 5 million, 3 bedrooms.” Repeat in Egyptian Arabic if helpful.
3. Show the matched Palm Residence with stored price, area, bedrooms, and availability. Choose an available time and book.
4. Open CRM and show the new qualified visitor, assigned agent, and conversation context.
5. Complete the viewing and show the post-viewing follow-up task. Evaluate due follow-ups and inspect the delivery queue. Explain the owner approval gate.
6. Record a deal, show commission-based revenue and conversion metrics, then show white-label company settings. Reset this fictional demo to replay.

## Operating cost model

For a small pilot, budget approximately USD 5–25/month for one persistent-disk application host, USD 0–20/month for backups/monitoring, plus a separately approved n8n plan after the trial. These are assumptions rather than vendor quotes. WhatsApp costs depend on current Meta pricing, destination, message category and templates; a BSP can add fees. AI cost is `requests × (average input tokens × current input price + average output tokens × current output price) / 1,000,000`. Enter current official prices and measured usage. The working rules assistant has no model API charge.

Support, client onboarding, data cleaning, uptime work, taxes, and currency movement are material costs. A production agreement needs explicit data handling, support hours, retention, service limits, and responsibility for channel approval. The product does not guarantee sales or investment outcomes.

## ROI calculator

The dashboard’s editable scenario uses `monthly leads × conversion increase in percentage points / 100 × commission per won deal − monthly operating cost`. Example: 100 leads × 2% × EGP 25,000 − EGP 2,500 = EGP 47,500 illustrative net value. This is a scenario, not a prediction. Compare measured before/after cohorts while accounting for seasonality and lead quality.
