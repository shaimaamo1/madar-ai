# Original MADAR demo illustrations

These 30 SVGs are original architectural vector illustrations created for MADAR AI, not photographs, scraped images or depictions of verified properties. They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No external image service, hotlink or attribution dependency is required. The generator is scripts/generate_demo_assets.py; each illustration labels itself DEMO and ORIGINAL ILLUSTRATION.
